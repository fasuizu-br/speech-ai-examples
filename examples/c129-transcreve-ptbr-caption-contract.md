# C129 · PT-BR caption QA contract

This is an opt-in, provider-neutral experiment for the Transcreve BR team.

## Job

Given an audio/video fixture that the operator owns or is authorized to process, compare transcript segments, speaker labels, and SRT/WebVTT timing before export.

## Boundaries

- No personal audio, private links, OAuth tokens, phone numbers, or credentials belong in this issue.
- A public route, visit, reply, or checkout redirect is not revenue.
- A paid buyer is counted only after payment, settlement, and bank reconciliation.
- The operator remains responsible for consent, deletion, and publication rights.

## Opt-in signal

Reply with the job, geography, authorized fixture type, and a measurable acceptance criterion. Do not paste private payloads.
