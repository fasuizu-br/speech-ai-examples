# C129 · PDF fidelity before RAG contract

This is an opt-in, provider-neutral experiment for PDF-to-Markdown and retrieval workflows.

## Job

Given a PDF fixture that the operator owns or is authorized to process, compare page references, tables, OCR text, and Markdown before indexing or sharing an answer.

## Boundaries

- No confidential documents, private links, credentials, or customer data belong in this issue.
- The artifact is a QA preflight, not a legal, tax, accounting, or compliance decision.
- A page view, export, or checkout redirect is not revenue.
- Revenue is counted only after a buyer pays and the payment is settled and bank-reconciled.

## Opt-in signal

Reply with document type, geography, authorized fixture type, and a measurable fidelity criterion. Do not paste private payloads.
