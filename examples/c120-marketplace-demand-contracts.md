# C120 marketplace demand contract (opt-in only)

This public example records a demand hypothesis for the 1% Lab C120 cycle. It is an invitation for maintainers and operators to review a bounded workflow, not an app-store submission or an unsolicited outreach campaign.

## Jobs in scope

- Ghost publishers and Twitch clip editors: authorized audio to transcript/SRT.
- Cloudflare Workers builders: a bounded speech API contract.
- SRE and PMO teams: incident or voice updates to reviewable evidence.
- RevOps teams: public-domain preflight before company enrichment.
- Data/procurement teams: PDF to Markdown fidelity QA before RAG.

## Public route

- [C120 Snowflake PDF→Markdown route](https://www.brainiall.com/transcreve/integracoes/c120-snowflake-pdf-rag)

The route uses a synthetic or authorized fixture, has no third-party OAuth, and does not claim Marketplace acceptance. Transcreve checkout is the only buyer-linked checkout shown; the other products remain qualification routes until their own payment and settlement evidence exists.

## Opt-in boundary

A maintainer may comment on the companion issue with the surface they want to test. Do not paste credentials, private documents, customer data, phone numbers, or tokens. No installation, account access, outbound message, or paid execution is implied by this file.
