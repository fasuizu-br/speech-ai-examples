# C128 · multigeo company-enrichment contract

BRAINIALL publishes a bounded, public-data workflow for company and supplier
resolution before CRM, procurement, or research enrichment.

## Audience and job

- Brazil: procurement and RevOps teams that need a CNPJ/domain candidate before
  opening a paid lookup.
- EU: finance and onboarding teams that need a VAT validation or LEI candidate
  with source provenance.
- UK/US/global: supplier, bid, compliance, and research operators that need a
  public registry identifier before a downstream workflow.

## Contract

1. Start with one public domain or registry identifier.
2. Use the local preflight to validate only the input shape.
3. Review the public source and the Actor result as a candidate, not as an
   official adverse-decision signal.
4. Use the first-party checkout or the public Actor only after the operator
   chooses the next step.

## Boundaries

No credentials, private customer data, CUI, personal data uploads, automated
credit/compliance decisions, or unsolicited outreach are requested. A visit,
preflight, Actor click, or reply is a demand signal only; it is not a purchase,
settlement, or revenue evidence.
